## Context

An approved candidate may become a repeated human process.

## Observation

Approval alone does not prove the process works in another context.

## Reusable guidance

Review scope, privacy, evidence, decision points, and rollback before promotion.

## Validation

Record an independent review and a successful dry run.

## Risks and rollback

If a new counterexample appears, mark the workflow superseded and link its replacement.
